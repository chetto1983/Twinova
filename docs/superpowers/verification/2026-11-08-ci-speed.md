## Timeouts made to fail fast (PR #12)

The 10 timeouts cost about 70 s of runner each. Each mutant was applied by hand to find the test that hung:

| Mutants | Why they hung | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `Bench::time_ns`/`scan_ns` → -1, `+` → `-` in `Engine::step` | `at()` cast a negative time with `as u64`: -1 ns became 584 years, which nexosim simulated scan by scan | `at()` refuses a negative time (a bug, now an error) |
| `reach`, `Belt::rebase` | the belt asked for an event every few ns: the tests ran out of time, and `drive` out of memory | `Belt::wake` asserts at most 1 000 + 10 000 events per simulated second (the free belt needs about 2) |
| `scene.rs` constants | a test ran for the belt's length over its speed, i.e. 10¹⁸ s | the scene's test helper refuses more than an hour |

Locally, `cargo mutants --in-place` on the former timeouts and on `Belt::wake`: 35 tested, 35 caught, none by timeout.
The two `reach` mutants now fail in 0.65 s.

caught, 10 unviable, **no timeout, none missed**. CI 42478809718 green, both bundles included.

## What this does not prove

- **The cause of the cache miss.** That the 10 GB repository limit evicted the `rust` cache is a hypothesis: the cache
  list is not readable from this session's proxy (HTTP 403).
- **Durations under load.** These are single runs on GitHub's shared runners.
