  completion commands amended to carry the editor's text).

- Machine: the cloud container with 4 cores, Linux, WebKitGTK under `xvfb-run -s "-screen 0 1440x900x24"`.

## What runs

- **The analysis** (`src-tauri/src/analysis.rs`):
  - truST's `BrowserAnalysisEngine` from `trust-wasm-analysis` at `5eb7f8c` (upstream v0.39.0), behind a managed
    `Mutex`;
  - `analyze` puts into one engine the program, the blocks as `components/<key>.st`, and the field's configuration
    (`lsp_files::FIELD`);
  - it drops W010 and W011, and an E202 that names a variable the configuration places;
  - `hover` and `completion` replace the file's document with the editor's text before answering.
- **The editor** (`StEditor.svelte`):
  - Monaco 0.57.0, the core with its hover, suggest, find, folding and editing contributions;
  - Monaco's own `st` language;
  - one model per tab; truST's diagnostics as markers, 300 ms after the last keystroke;
  - the Problems pane lists them before any Build, and a row opens its line.

### Acceptance 1: the editor against trust-lsp 0.39.0

**Method:**
- A probe test saved each valid fixture with the app's commands, plus the diverter with the broken program (E203, E101
  and E205), in a folder with Twinova's `trust-lsp.toml` and `field.st`.
- On each folder it wrote what `analyze` returns, from the app with that project open.
- `lsp_probe.py` opened every `.st` file in `trust-lsp` 0.39.0, built in release from the tag's commit `5eb7f8c`, and
  recorded what it published and what a pull returned. The two are equal in every case.
- `acc47_compare.py` compared the editor's list with the server's, file, line, column and code. The generated
  `.twinova/lsp/field.st` was left out: the editor does not show it.

| Case | Editor | trust-lsp, less what the filter drops | Dropped: E202 in blocks | W010, W011 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `code_sort_line` | 25 | 25 | 17 | 0 | equal |
| `curve_line` | 9 | 9 | 9 | 0 | equal |
| `demo_m3` | 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 | equal |
| `merge_line` | 5 | 5 | 3 | 0 | equal |
| `sort_line` | 23 | 23 | 17 | 0 | equal |
| `two_belts_diverter` | 8 | 8 | 8 | 0 | equal |
| broken diverter | 11 | 11 | 8 | 0 | equal |

- **What was dropped.** Every dropped E202 is a block's `AT %I*` or `%Q*` variable. The field configuration places all
  of them for every instance.
- **Run again on 2026-11-13,** after the 4.4a line gained its infeed photocell and its gap-control program (pull
  request #44): 7 cases out of 7 equal. The program's warnings and errors moved down with the new declarations (W009
  at 11:9, the W001 at 14:5, 21:5 and 22:5; the broken program's errors at 37:1, 38:1 and 39:19).
- **No W010 or W011 was published:** Twinova's `trust-lsp.toml` turns the nondeterminism warnings off.
- **The first run was wrong, and showed it.** The probe had analysed with an app holding no project, so without the
  configuration and the filter. Each block then showed its E202 and a W009.

## What this does not prove

- **That the frontend at 0.39.0 and the VM at `6405915` agree on every construct.** Build stays the referee.
- **Windows,** until CI's windows-2022 E2E on the pull request.
- **Typing through the keyboard:** WebKitWebDriver drops typed spaces, so the E2E sets the text through the editor.
- **The CPU of a soak shorter than a minute per phase, on one machine.** It is a comparison of two runs, not a
  benchmark.
- **Definition, references, rename, breakpoints and online monitoring.** They are the next phases, from research `17`.
