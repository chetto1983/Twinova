## What this does not prove

- **Snapshots across sessions or program edits**: one in-memory slot, for the program it was taken with.
- **Real time**: the lag is shown, not prevented. On the CI runners and here, ×1 and ×2 kept within 1 %, which is not a
  guarantee on a loaded machine.
- **Long snapshots**: a restore replays from zero, linear in the snapshot's time (about 19 s for an 8 h shift, by
  extrapolation of the one-hour figure).
- **That every mutant is caught.** `mutation.yml` on `main` at `b0221f9` (run 42467342248):
  - **Counts:** 219 mutants; 194 caught, 7 missed, 8 timeouts, 10 unviable.
  - **Score:** 96.5 % of the viable mutants that finished, above CLAUDE.md's 70 %.

    mutants), see `src/outcome.rs` lines 110–122, and the gate accepted 3. The survivors were:
    - in `belt.rs`: `rebase`, `motor_is`, `next_event` and `photocell_at`;
    - in `bench.rs`: `BeltModel::init` and `schedule`;
