# Plan: the editor, second part — snapping by interfaces (ROADMAP 4.5b)

## Goal

In the app, the parts of a line snap together:
- conveyors end to end, with a gap the user sets;
- devices onto a conveyor's rail;
- sources and sinks onto ends;
- spurs onto a straight's flank.

Each snap is one undo step, and the ROADMAP's line with a diverter is built by hand with drag and snap only.

## Architecture

- **`twinova-physics` gains the geometry the editor needs:**
  - `layout.rs` keeps its rules, with one conveyor geometry per instance extracted into `conveyor.rs`;
  - `interfaces.rs`: each instance's ends and rail;
  - `snap.rs`: `join`, `merge` and `moved`, over `twinova_project::edit`.
- **A belt's deck reaches its drum faces** (`Belt::face`, 85 mm for `twinova.belt/1`, 0 otherwise). The fixtures are
  re-spaced by a script.
- **`twinova-project` gains the `merge` connection.** It is validated, encoded, and removed with its instances.
- **The app and the agent depend on `twinova-physics` directly:**
  - the app's `edit` command gains `join` and `merge`, and its `move` goes through `moved`;
  - the view carries each instance's interfaces;
  - the agent gains a `join` tool.
- **The webview:**
  - `snapping.ts` finds the best candidate for a pose;
  - `sceneEditing.ts` shows it while dragging and commits it on release;
  - `SnapBar.svelte` holds the toggle and the gap;
  - the inspector edits a flow's gap.

## Global Constraints

- **The running line changes only at Build.** An edit never touches the engine.
- **One gesture is one undo step:** the move, the height, the flow and the flows a move breaks.
- **Clean-room.**
  - OIP's snap is read for ideas, at `0572868`.
  - Its thresholds (0.3 m, a quarter turn, 45°, 0.6 m, 0.5 per radian, 0.15 m) and its belt face (half the 0.17 m belt
    height) are cited where used.
  - No code is copied from it.
- **One geometry.** The ends come from the function that builds the physics' conveyors, never from a second copy in
  TypeScript. The view composes the local frames it is sent.
- **Files:** none over 600 lines.
  - `layout.rs` (409 lines) gives its conveyor to `conveyor.rs`.
  - `App.svelte` (389 lines) gives the snap controls to `SnapBar.svelte`.
- **Fixtures** are written by a script independent of the encoder and of `snap.rs` (`tests/corpus.rs`). The re-spacing
  is that script's formula.
- **Gates:**
  - fmt, clippy;
  - coverage ≥ 85 %;
  - cargo mutants on `conveyor`, `interfaces`, `snap`, the belt's deck and the merge's layout, with no survivor;
  - Stryker ≥ 70 % with `snapping.ts` added;
  - svelte-check, oxlint, knip, jscpd, i18n;
  - the E2E on Linux and Windows.

## Review Focus

- **The belt's faces.**
  - Only `twinova.belt/1` changes: decks and guards reach −85 mm and `length` + 85 mm.
  - A mount's `at`, a source's drop and a sensor's beam keep their place along the run.
  - Each re-pinned fingerprint and timing says why.
- **`moved` only removes a join that breaks:** faces more than 0.15 m apart, 1 mm off one axis or 0.1° apart. It never
  adds a join.
- **Build accepts any gap in a flow.** It refuses a merge that is not square, or whose face is more than 0.15 m from the
  flank.
- **The view's candidate and Rust's join agree.** The view only names the pair; Rust computes the pose.

## Decisions taken while writing this plan

1. **`Belt::face`** is how far the deck and guards reach past each end of the run, in metres.
   - `layout` sets it to 0.085 for `twinova.belt/1`: OIP's `BELT_HEIGHT / 2`, as `families.ts` draws it.
   - It is 0 for the other families. The physics tests' `belt()` helper keeps 0, so their numbers do not move.
2. **End frames are local and in f64**, their origin on the face at the running surface, with x out of the part. The view
   receives them in `ProjectView.interfaces`, keyed by instance.
3. **The heights of decision 2** are solved in closed form. An end's surface height is affine in `height` (slope 1),
   inclined or not: the mover's `height` += target end y − mover end y.
4. **A turntable's rims** are named `left` and `right`: across its lane at its start heading, 0.75 widths out, as
   `path.rs` caps it.
5. **A merge's `side`** is `"left"` or `"right"` of the host's flow, as a pusher's `out_right`.
6. **Source and sink poses.** A joined source or sink stands at the face, on the floor (y 0), heading along the flow.
   Its own pose stays cosmetic to the physics.
7. **The gap field** is per session, 0 at start, from 0 to 150 mm in millimetres. The inspector writes a flow's gap with
   `join` on the selection.
8. **Alt during a drag** suppresses candidates; the commit is then a plain move.
