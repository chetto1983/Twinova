# Plan: roller conveyor, chain transfer and the first complete line (ROADMAP 4.4a)

## Goal

M4.1's acceptance line runs in the app. A source feeds belt A, then a roller conveyor with OIP's chain transfer fitted
inside it, then belt B and the normal sink. The transfer's side exit leads to a reject sink. The physics and native
models are translated from OIP into Rust; the 3D geometry is copied from OIP in TypeScript.

## Architecture

- **`twinova-project`:**
  - `twinova.roller@1` and `twinova.chain_transfer@1` in the fixtures;
  - `twinova.diverter` goes;
  - the chain transfer mounts on a roller conveyor.
- **`twinova-physics`:**
  - roller conveyors are static decks with their own grip;
  - a chain transfer is a kinematic deck, moved before each step;
  - exits (a conveyor's end, a transfer's side) carry sinks and walls.
- **`field.rs` / `line.rs`:**
  - `ChainTransferBehaviour` (ST) exposes `lift` and `raised`;
  - the line moves the chain deck every millisecond.
- **The view:**
  - OIP's geometry kernel and family generators, copied;
  - an adapter from mesh descriptions to three.js;
  - the snapshot carries each transfer's lift.

## Tech Stack

Rust (Rapier 0.36.1 with `enhanced-determinism`, truST `6405915`), Tauri 2, Svelte 5, three.js 0.187; cargo test,
vitest, Stryker, cargo mutants; E2E with tauri-driver on Linux and Windows.

## Spec

`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-diverter-line-design.md` (approved 2026-11-10).

## Global Constraints

- **Provenance:**
  - every copied or translated file starts with its OIP source path and commit `1e7488c`;
  - `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` carries OIP's MIT notice.
- **Determinism:**
  - the replay tests stay green at every task;
  - the physics fingerprint changes only in Task 2. The demo has no roller, so its fingerprint must not change;
    whatever changes is shown in the commit.
- **Gates:**
  - 600 LOC per file, copied files included;
  - coverage ≥ 85 %;
  - zero surviving mutants in `mutants.toml`'s scope;
  - Stryker ≥ 70 % on the new view helpers;
  - knip and jscpd clean.
- **The app works at every commit.** `demo_m3` keeps running throughout.
- **Fixtures are written by a script** independent of the Rust encoder (`tests/corpus.rs`).

## Review Focus

- The kinematic deck: moved before the step, never colliding with static decks, and its surface velocity sign when it
  is collider 2.
- No carton lost or duplicated at the side exit.
- Translations faithful to OIP's constants. OIP's Jolt-only gates are left out, and the measurement that left them
  out is shown.

## Decisions taken while writing this plan

- **The chain transfer has no flow input.** Cartons reach it on its host, so its only flow port is `out`, the side
  exit. Its mount ties it to the host.
- **The transfer's deck is placed in the host's frame,** at the mount position along the host, centred across it.
