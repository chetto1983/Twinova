# Plan: the in-app ST editor (ROADMAP 4.7)

## Goal

- **The editor:** the PLC pane edits Structured Text in Monaco, with:
  - highlighting;
  - diagnostics while typing, as VS Code shows them, without the false E202;
  - hover and completion, from truST's analysis engine at v0.39.0.
- **The frontend `rev` moves** as the 2.6 policy says, and M2 closes.

## Architecture

- **Rust, `src/analysis.rs`.**
  - A `BrowserAnalysisEngine` from `trust-wasm-analysis` at `5eb7f8c` (v0.39.0), in a managed `Mutex`.
  - The commands `analyze`, `hover` and `completion`.
  - A pure filter: no W010 or W011, and no E202 where the field's configuration places the variable.
  - One analysis project: the program, the blocks, and the field's generated configuration.
- **The webview.**
  - `Editor.svelte` mounts Monaco 0.57.0, the editor core and its worker, in place of the textarea.
  - Monaco's own `st` language highlights (spec decision 2, amended).
  - `analysis.ts` holds the debounce, the markers, and the hover and completion providers.
  - The Problems pane lists the analysis's diagnostics, then Build's.
- **The runtime stays at `6405915`** (2.6).

## Tech Stack

- Rust 2024: the app, with `trust-wasm-analysis` 0.39.0.
- Svelte 5, `monaco-editor` 0.57.0 (MIT), Vite workers.

## Spec

`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-st-editor-design.md` (approved 2026-11-12, decisions 1 to 7).

## Global Constraints

- **The 4.6 behaviour is unchanged:** edited tabs, Revert, held disk changes, online changes.
- **Build stays the referee.** The editor's diagnostics never block Build.
- **Clean-room.** truST's engine is MIT OR Apache-2.0 and cited where used; Monaco, its `st` language included, is MIT.
- **Files:** none over 600 lines. `Editor.svelte` (283 lines) gives Monaco's setup to `monacoEditor.ts`.
- **Gates:**
  - fmt, clippy;
  - coverage ≥ 85 %;
  - cargo mutants on `analysis.rs`;
  - Stryker ≥ 70 % on `analysis.ts`;
  - svelte-check, oxlint, knip, jscpd, i18n;
  - the E2E on Linux and windows-2022.

## Review Focus

- **The E202 filter** drops only "requires VAR_CONFIG mapping" on a variable the generated configuration places for
  every instance of its block.
- **Positions:** the engine's `{line, character}` against Monaco's 1-based line and column (Task 2 measures the
  unit).
- **Two copies of the frontend crates** in the build. The runtime never sees the new ones.

## Decisions taken while writing this plan

1. **How the E2E reaches Monaco.** The editor sets its instance on its host element (`host.editor`). The E2E reads
   and writes the text through it:
   - `document.querySelector(".monaco-host").editor.getValue()` / `setValue()`;
   - no global, and no flag that differs between builds.

   WebKitWebDriver drops typed spaces (measured), so keys cannot be the way in.
2. **The debounce is 300 ms** (spec decision 6). `analyze` sends every source, because the texts are small (the
   largest fixture's sources are under 10 KB).
3. **No tokenizer of our own.** Monaco's `st` covers Twinova's sources, all but the `*` of `%I*` (measured; spec
   decision 2, amended after the user pointed to it).
4. **Two Monaco themes** (light, dark) take their colours from the Aura tokens' computed values at mount, and again
   when the app's theme changes.
5. **The 148 frontend commits** from the pin to `5eb7f8c` are listed in the PR's body, as the 2.6 policy says, and the
   verification file keeps the list.
